- Status: completed; provider pair parked
- Owner: researcher and Agent
- Gate: G-002 prospective recruitability
- Claims: C-000
- Candidate line: L-000
- Lane: integrate/prospective preparation
- Resource cap: no controller training; provider execution only after a sealed
  fresh-cohort protocol and resource ledger

## Decision to unlock

Decide whether the frozen abstention-then-disagreement policy reproduces
provider complement and cost-efficient recruitment on a fresh blinded cohort.

## Context

Earlier work established excellent replay and cost accounting but repeatedly
failed when a narrow repair language was asked to turn raster disagreement into
correct programs.  Separately frozen strong providers already appear to reach
different exact candidate regions.  `RDR-0001` therefore changes the unit of
control from a hand-written repair action to provider recruitment over a
content-addressed population.

## Non-goals

- No new model training or candidate generation.
- No policy fitting, threshold search, or controller comparison.
- No claim that the exposed ARC-TGI result is prospective confirmation.
- No presentation or publication polishing.

## Stop and escalation rules

Stop if shared tasks do not have exact query input identities, if any plan
construction path reads recruited candidates or solutions, if replay
identities differ, if fresh visual complement is below 3/100, or at the
declared cap.  A failed gate is evidence against the frozen policy, not
permission to tune it on the same cohort.

## Closure

`G-002` closed as provider-null: VARC contributed two exclusive tasks, below
the minimum of three, and all three marginal tasks came from one sealed source
family.  The frozen ranking recovered all three descriptively, but this does
not unlock final selection (`C-003`).  The NVARC+VARC pair is parked for
confirmation; the next candidate must add family-diverse coverage rather than
a learned router.
