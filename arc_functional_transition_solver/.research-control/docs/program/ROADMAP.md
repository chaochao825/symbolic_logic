# Program Roadmap

Updated: 2026-08-12

## Horizon 0: normalize the heterogeneous population

- Reproduce the exposed recursive 92, visual 89, and union 95 under one
  query-gold-free, content-addressed population envelope.
- Preserve provider-specific rank, support, provenance, and cost identities.
- Confirm deterministic replay and reject forged or mismatched freezes.

Exit gate: complement is separated from repair performance and represented by
one auditable artifact.

## Horizon 1: test query-blind recruitability

- Freeze one cheap score from task structure and recursive candidate-state
  diagnostics, before invoking the visual provider.
- Compare 10%, 20%, 30%, and 50% visual budgets against deterministic random,
  fixed order, and equal-cost restart.
- Measure exclusive-task recall, union gain recovered, native cost, and
  coverage per compute; do not train a neural controller.

Exit gate: either a pre-provider signal recovers at least half of complement at
30% budget, or recruitment is narrowed as unidentifiable from available state.

## Horizon 2: prospective replication and final selection

- Generate a fresh, family-disjoint cohort and freeze the chosen policy.
- Test pass@2 selection from the recruited population, not only oracle union.
- Test cost intervention, provider lesion, and insertion of a new provider.

Exit gate: `C-000` is supported, narrowed, or rejected under prospective native
cost and final-output metrics.

## Horizon 3: procedural memory and bounded repair

- Promote verified programs/concepts into reusable procedural memory.
- Reactivate typed residual repair only for certified near-misses where its
  expected marginal value exceeds restart.
- Consider bandit, XGBoost, recurrent, then diffusion control only in that order.
