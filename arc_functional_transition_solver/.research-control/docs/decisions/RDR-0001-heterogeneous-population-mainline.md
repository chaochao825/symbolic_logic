# RDR-0001: Use heterogeneous hypothesis populations as the mainline

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-12
- Decider: researcher

## Context

Repeated controller and typed-repair experiments were valid negative results.
Weak symbolic pools had too little oracle coverage for routing to matter, while
visual raster disagreement was predictive of error but narrow pixel, relation,
and effect languages produced no unique repair.  In contrast, independently
frozen reference-scale providers on the same exposed ARC-TGI cohort reached
different exact candidate regions: recursive 92/100, visual 89/100, and union
95/100.

## Decision

The sole mainline is budgeted functional recruitment over a content-addressed
population of heterogeneous provider hypotheses.

Providers retain their own representations, training procedures, internal
search, and ranking.  The shared workspace normalizes only task/query identity,
candidate output, provenance, verification state, and native cost.  The online
controller decides which specialist to invoke next from query-blind state and
expected marginal validated value per cost.

Typed residual repair remains available only as a near-miss operator.  It is
not the default bridge between providers and cannot be reactivated merely by
adding another narrow hand-written relation.  Learned controllers remain
frozen until a non-neural recruitment policy demonstrates a causal signal.

## Consequences

- Provider complement is tested before controller quality.
- Candidate freeze and scoring remain separate gold boundaries.
- Population-level union, exclusive coverage, recruitment recall, final pass@2,
  and native cost are distinct metrics.
- The existing 92/89/95 cohort is development evidence because its outcome is
  exposed; it can validate implementation but not prospectively confirm the
  claim.
- The brain-inspired claim is limited to functional specialization, shared
  workspace state, selective recruitment, lesions, cost interventions, and
  procedural memory.  No biological neural equivalence is claimed.
