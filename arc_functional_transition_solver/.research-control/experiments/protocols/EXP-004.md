## Primary question

On the collision-free 98-task subset of the sealed reserve, do independently
rerun recursive and visual providers retain strict candidate complement, and
does the unchanged anchor-abstention-then-disagreement policy recover that
complement under a fixed 30-task activation budget?

This is a new experiment after `EXP-003`; it does not repair or reinterpret the
invalid 100-task run.  The two exclusions are determined solely from blind
demonstration and query inputs, before either provider is run and without
opening query outputs.

## Frozen sequence and leakage boundary

1. Verify the eligible seal, parent binding, and absence of any reserve
   solution artifact.
2. Compile and audit the 98-task recursive dataset without a solution file.
3. Run and freeze the recursive candidate pool and native-cost receipt twice.
4. Freeze the sorted task universe and
   `anchor-abstention-then-disagreement/v2` plan before the visual candidate is
   available.  The priority key remains, in order: anchor abstention; minimum
   top-output support ascending; mean non-top support descending; mean unique
   candidates descending; mean output-shape count descending; task ID.
5. Commit the plan identity, 10/20/30/49-task prefixes, and 256 content-hash
   random orders before visual execution.
6. Run and freeze the visual candidate pool and native-cost receipt twice.
7. Freeze the heterogeneous population.  Oracle authorization must bind the
   eligible seal, both candidate-freeze IDs, population ID, and plan ID before
   deterministic oracle regeneration is permitted.
8. Score provider complement first.  If that gate fails, record the provider
   null and keep recruitment descriptive.
9. If complement passes, score the fixed recruitment plan and native-cost
   controls.  Do not train or tune a controller.

## Gates

### G-002a: candidate complement

The visual provider must contribute at least 3/98 strict task-level exclusive
hits over the recursive anchor.  Also report anchor-exclusive hits, union
increment, query-level composition, abstentions, exact output overlap, and all
neither-hit tasks.

Failure parks `C-001` for this provider pair and makes recruitment descriptive
only.  It is not permission to alter either provider or threshold on this
cohort.

### G-002b: functional recruitment

At the predeclared 30-task prefix, the frozen policy must:

- recover at least two anchor-miss/union-hit tasks;
- recover at least half of all anchor-miss/union-hit tasks;
- strictly exceed the upper median of the 256 frozen random task-count orders;
- consume no more than 35% of the full visual provider's native GPU seconds.

Report 10-, 20-, 30-, and 49-task curves.  Also compare with each deterministic
random order truncated by the same observed native GPU-second budget.  This
runtime-matched analysis is secondary because task runtimes become known only
after execution.

Passing both gates partially supports `C-002` and permits a separate frozen
experiment on final pass@2 selection (`C-003`).  It does not permit a learned
router.

## Invalidity conditions

- Any query output is materialized or opened before both candidate freezes,
  population, and plan pass content-identity checks.
- Plan construction reads visual candidates, provider scores, query
  solutions, or marginal-value labels.
- The recursive boundary audit is not clean or either provider consumes query
  gold.
- Shared query inputs, task universe, provider source, checkpoint,
  configuration, or replay identity differs from this contract.
- A shard changes the single-task semantics or cannot produce an independently
  auditable native-cost receipt.
- An unrelated process is displaced from a GPU.

## Resource cap and stop rule

- One reference-scale NVARC run on one idle A800.
- One complete 98-task VARC evaluation; each task retains the existing 2,400 s
  timeout.  At most three isolated single-GPU shards may use otherwise idle
  GPUs.
- No model pretraining, controller training, provider tuning, threshold
  search, or post-outcome feature changes.
- Stop immediately on an invalidity condition, failed provider receipt, or
  complement failure after authorized oracle opening.

## Outcome mapping

- Confirmatory positive: `G-002a` and `G-002b` both pass.
- Mechanism null: complement passes but recruitment fails.
- Provider null: complement fails.
- Boundary: gates pass, but prior demo/query-input exposure or the secondary
  observed-runtime comparison limits inference.
- Invalid: a leakage, identity, replay, source, execution, or cost-accounting
  condition fails.

## Recorded outcome

The result is recorded in `.research-control/results/RESULT-EXP-004.md`.
