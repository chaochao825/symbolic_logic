# EXP-001: Anchor-only functional recruitment diagnostic

- Status: frozen
- Date: 2026-08-12
- Gate: G-001
- Claim: C-002
- Candidate line: L-000
- Lane: explore

## Question

Before reading visual candidates or solutions, can disagreement within the
recursive anchor freeze prioritize the rare tasks where invoking the visual
provider increases strict population coverage?

## Evidence status and independence

- Evidence tier: post-hoc development diagnostic on an outcome-exposed cohort.
- Independent unit: whole task; recruitment occurs once per task.
- Selection boundary: the task priority plan is frozen from only the NVARC/TRM
  candidate freeze, its cost receipt, this protocol, and task IDs.
- Scoring boundary: VARC candidates, population-result task hits, solutions, and
  per-task VARC runtime are opened only after the plan exists.
- No result from this experiment can confirm `C-002`; it only decides whether a
  fresh prospective provider run is justified.

## Frozen policy

For every task and query, reconstruct support counts over the anchor's ten
ranked attempts.  Order tasks lexicographically by:

1. minimum top-output support across queries, ascending;
2. mean non-top support, descending;
3. mean number of unique candidates, descending;
4. mean number of output shapes, descending;
5. task ID, ascending.

All ratios are compared as exact integer fractions.  No fitted weight, outcome
label, visual posterior, task solution, or floating-point score is permitted.

Freeze prefix budgets of 10%, 20%, 30%, and 50%.  Also freeze 256 uniform
content-hash priority orders using consecutive seeds 0--255.  The 30% prefix is
primary; the other prefixes describe the budget curve and cannot replace it.

## Metrics and controls

- Marginal-value task: recursive provider misses the strict task while the full
  recursive-plus-visual population union hits it.
- Primary metric: marginal-value task recall at 30 visual activations.
- Secondary metrics: strict coverage after recruitment, all budget-prefix
  recalls, 256-seed random recovery distribution, and observed selected VARC
  GPU seconds relative to the full VARC run.
- Cost matching: selected task count is primary because every VARC task reserves
  the same 101-epoch protocol and timeout; observed GPU seconds are reported as
  a secondary non-controlled quantity.
- Equal-cost restart is not available from the frozen artifacts and therefore
  is not claimed by this development diagnostic.

## Acceptance threshold

At the 30% prefix, recover at least 2 of the 3 exposed marginal-value tasks,
recover at least half of all marginal-value tasks, and strictly exceed the
upper median recovery of the 256 pre-frozen random orders.

Passing permits a separately preregistered fresh-cohort replication.  It does
not support a learned router or a final pass@2 claim.

## Falsifier

Recovering zero or one marginal-value task at 30%, or failing to exceed the
random upper median, rejects this anchor-disagreement policy as a useful
recruitment signal on the exposed cohort.  Do not tune weights or add features
on these same outcomes after failure.

## Invalidity conditions

- The plan reads a visual candidate freeze, population score, solution, or
  task-level provider outcome.
- Plan and provider task/query identities differ.
- Plan, full result, or compact summary fails byte-identical A/B replay.
- A provider run receipt is forged, incomplete, or not query-gold-blind.

## Resource cap and stop rule

CPU-only, 30 minutes, no model or provider rerun, no controller training, and
exactly one policy plus its pre-frozen random controls.  Stop after two replay
passes or on any invalidity condition.

## Outcome mapping

- Valid-positive: the development acceptance threshold passes.
- Null: the valid primary prefix recovers zero or one task or ties random.
- Boundary: the primary prefix passes but observed native runtime materially
  exceeds its reserved task fraction.
- Invalid: a gold/visual path enters plan construction or identity checks fail.
